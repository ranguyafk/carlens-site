Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

CarLens ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the CarLens app, website, and related services.

Information We Collect

When you use CarLens, we may collect the following information:

  • Email addresses used for account creation, authentication, analytics, and communication.
  • Profile information such as usernames, profile photos, biographies, and uploaded content.
  • Community posts, comments, uploaded images, and interactions with other users.
  • Device information including device type, operating system, app version, IP address, and identifiers.
  • Analytics and usage data such as features used, pages viewed, crashes, engagement, and session activity.
  • Photos or images uploaded for AI vehicle recognition purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide AI-powered vehicle recognition features.
  • Create and manage user accounts and profiles.
  • Display community posts and user-generated content tied to your account.
  • Improve app performance, features, moderation, and user experience.
  • Analyze usage trends and app analytics.
  • Communicate updates, support responses, and service notifications.
  • Help personalize content and advertisements shown within the app.

Advertising

CarLens may display advertisements through third-party advertising partners. These partners may use device identifiers, cookies, analytics data, or approximate location information to provide personalized or relevant advertisements.

We do not sell personal information directly, but advertising providers may collect certain data through their own technologies and policies.

Community Content

Information you post publicly, including usernames, profile pictures, vehicle uploads, comments, and community posts, may be visible to other users.

Your account may be associated with your posts, activity, collections, and uploaded content.

AI Recognition Data

Images uploaded to CarLens for AI recognition may be temporarily processed and analyzed to identify vehicles and improve recognition accuracy.

We may use anonymized or aggregated data to improve AI systems and app performance.

Analytics

We may use third-party analytics providers to understand how users interact with CarLens. This may include app activity, device information, performance metrics, and engagement statistics.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user data, however no online platform or storage system can guarantee complete security. Use of the app is at your own risk.

Children's Privacy

CarLens is not intended for children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of CarLens after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
